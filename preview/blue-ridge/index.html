Comprehensive Insurance Products

Blue Ridge Specialty offers a full suite of coverages designed specifically for the transportation industry and beyond.

Auto Liability

The foundation of any transportation insurance program. We offer robust primary limits to protect motor carriers against third-party bodily injury and property damage claims.

Physical Damage

Comprehensive and collision coverage tailored to protect tractors, trailers, and specialized equipment from physical loss or damage.

Motor Truck Cargo

Flexible protection for the freight you haul. Our cargo policies can cover specific commodities or broad goods tailored to the exact needs of your operation.

Truck General Liability

Essential coverage for premises and operations risks outside of driving, including loading/unloading and terminal operations.

Contingent Cargo

Crucial protection for freight brokers and forwarders handling cargo transit, providing secondary coverage if a primary carrier's policy fails to respond.

Contractor's Equipment

Broad coverage for mobile machinery, tools, and equipment used in contractor operations, whether on the job site or in transit.

Excess Motor Truck Cargo

Additional layers of cargo protection designed to satisfy higher shipper requirements for high-value or specialized loads.

Warehouse Legal Liability

Coverage for logistics providers and warehousemen protecting against liability for damage to third-party goods stored at their facilities.

Business Auto

Commercial auto solutions for local business fleets, HVAC companies, electrical contractors, and other artisan trades.

Livery & Charter Buses

Specialized liability and physical damage coverage designed for the unique risks of passenger transportation services.

Scalable Solutions for Any Size Risk

No account is too small, and no fleet is too complex. Whether you are placing coverage for a single-unit owner-operator or a large, sophisticated commercial fleet, Blue Ridge Specialty delivers the tailored solutions you need to keep your clients moving forward.
